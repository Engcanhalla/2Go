If people are told what others around them are doing, they will often copy it, since going along with the crowd feels safe. A sign that warns of a fine pressures people, whereas one noting that others already take their trash home gently nudges them. Such nudges do not always work, though. In a study of home energy use, residents were shown how their consumption compared with their neighbors' averages. Those who had been using more than average cut back, exactly as expected, but households that had been using less increased their use once they learned they were below average.
